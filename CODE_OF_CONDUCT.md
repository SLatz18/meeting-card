# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone taking part in the project's issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

To report a conduct problem, contact the maintainer, [@SLatz18](https://github.com/SLatz18), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may block participants who do so repeatedly, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
